MIDI Note Shuffler

Pick a range of notes and get a random arrangement of them, with no note played twice in a row, note lengths you control and room for gaps. Useful for triggering samplers, slicers and drum racks, or just for shaking loose new ideas. Click a note name in the roll to leave it out.

"Save this .mid" saves straight to Downloads. "Save variations" saves a .zip; double-click it in Finder to get the .mid files. Drag one onto a MIDI track in your DAW at bar 1.

Note names follow Logic and Ableton, where middle C (MIDI 60) is C3. Some apps call that note C4; the notes are the same, only the label differs. Same seed and settings always give the same pattern, so a seed you like is worth writing down.
